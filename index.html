Got questions?

      

        

          
Reader mail

          
Straight answers, including the uncomfortable ones.

        

        

          

            Q.01Does GULP ever move my funds?
            
No. GULP only asks your wallet to sign a text message. It never requests a transaction. If a pop-up ever asks you to approve a transaction, close it.

          

          

            Q.02What does "absorbed" mean?
            
When holders approve a nomination, the coin is marked absorbed in the public ledger. The team announces the next step for that coin's community on X.

          

          

            Q.03Who can vote, and how much?
            
Any wallet that holds $GULP. Your weight is your on-chain balance, read from the Solana network. Before launch, each wallet counts as one vote.

          

          

            Q.04Where do the numbers come from?
            
Liquidity, volume and pool age come from DexScreener. Supply and wallet balances come from the Solana network. Nothing is typed in by hand.

          

          

            Q.05Are votes stored on-chain?
            
Not yet. Signed votes are kept in your browser, and mirrored to the team's server once one is connected. The signature proves which wallet cast it.

          

          

            Q.06Can a coin that still trades be nominated?
            
No. If it is too active or too young, the verdict says Not allowed and tells you why. Only quiet coins get a vote.

How it works
Four steps from a quiet coin to the ledger.

    

      

        
01
Find
Spot a coin that has gone quiet. No trades, no liquidity.

        
02
Paste
Drop its address into the checker. You get a verdict and the reason.

        
03
Vote
Holders vote yes or no. Quorum and the clock are shown on every case.

        
04
Absorbed
A winning case goes in the ledger. A losing one is kept there too.

      

      

        

          
Key protections

          
    
            
  • Signature only. No transaction is ever requested.
    • 
            
  • Live coins and brand-new coins cannot be nominated.
    • 
            
  • One vote per wallet per case.
    • 
            
  • Weight is read from the chain, not typed in.
    • 
            
  • Rejected cases stay in the ledger.
    • 
          

        

        

          
The honest risk box

          
    
            
  • Meme coins are volatile and can go to zero.
    • 
            
  • A verdict is a reading of pool numbers, not advice.
    • 
            
  • Votes are stored in the browser until a server is connected.
    • 
            
  • Never share a seed phrase with anyone, including us.
